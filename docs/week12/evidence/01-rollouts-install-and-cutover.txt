## reading
   First sync: the Rollout stayed InvalidSpec ("Canary DestinationRule subset 'canary' not found in
   route"). The live VirtualServices still had the Week 10 split (subsets v1 90 / v2 10), and the new
   ignoreDifferences on route weights + RespectIgnoreDifferences made Argo CD keep the live route
   array instead of applying git's stable/canary route. One-time migration: the two VirtualServices
   (catalog, shop-edge) rendered from git were applied with kubectl; Argo CD then reported Synced.
   Cut-over without an outage: until the new pods were ready the stable subset (labels
   app.kubernetes.io/name=catalog only) matched the old Deployment pods; Argo Rollouts then pinned
   both subsets to rollouts-pod-template-hash 6b495dc9d and Argo CD pruned the two Deployments last.
   A first deployment of a Rollout is not a canary (nothing to compare with): 1.2.0 went to 100 % directly.
